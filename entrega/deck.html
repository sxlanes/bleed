The solution

Paste a URL. Get the bill.

One input, one minute, no account. Bleed crawls the site, works out which leaks matter for that specific business, prices each one against calibrated constants, and writes the case in language the owner actually speaks.

  • MeasuredStack, order path, load time, image weight, aggregator links, open catalogue.
  • JudgedA model decides which leaks matter for this business and why, here.
  • PricedDeterministic arithmetic over constants that carry their source and date.
  • ArguedA one-page dossier for a non-technical owner, with the assumptions attached.
The model judges. The code calculates. Neither does the other's job.

Who it is for

Two users, one report

  • The ownerRuns one to three venues. Not technical. Knows something is wrong with delivery margins but has no way to size it. Wants a number and a next step, not an audit.
  • The agencySells web work to local business. Currently opens with "your site is old". Bleed hands them an opening that is a euro figure with a citation, and a dossier to leave behind.
  • Not forEnterprise. Chains with in-house teams. Anyone who already has the number.
The wedge is restaurants with delivery. Other trades are roadmap, not claim.

What it does today

Shipped, not mocked

  • ReconLive HTTP crawl: stack detection, WooCommerce Store API, schema.org hours, aggregator links, image weight, time to first byte.
  • TriageStructured-output model pass that ranks the leaks that apply to this business and says why they matter here.
  • QuantifySeven leak types priced from seven calibrated constants. Numbers with no published source are labelled on screen as team estimates.
  • SimulateThe owner edits the assumptions live and the report recalculates. Their numbers beat our averages.
  • DossierGenerated brief, with a deterministic fallback when the model is unavailable.

Architecture

Four stages, two engines

1. recon
deterministic
Fetch, parse, measure. Facts only, no interpretation.
2. triage
gemini-3.6-flash
Structured output. Picks and ranks leaks from a closed catalogue. Returns judgement, never euros.
3. quantify
deterministic
Arithmetic over cited constants. Auditable, repeatable, no hallucination surface.
4. dossier
gemini-3.6-flash
Writes the owner's case. Falls back to a template if the model is unavailable.

The split is the design decision. Letting a model invent revenue figures is a hallucination waiting to be quoted back at a real business. Letting it judge what matters is exactly what it is good at. Every model output is validated against a closed catalogue before it is used.

Where the AI is, and where it is not

Honest about the boundary

  • Model doesReads the business from its own site. Decides which of the leaks apply here and ranks them. Explains why each matters for this venue specifically. Writes the owner's brief.
  • Code doesEvery measurement. Every euro. Every citation. Structured-output validation that rejects any leak the model invents.
  • Nobody doesGuess. When a figure needs the owner's own data, the report says so instead of filling it in.

Two of the constants proposed during development were rejected because their only sources were vendors selling the fix. That rule is enforced in the codebase, not just claimed on a slide.

Fallback path tested: with no API key the product still works end to end

Impact and business

A number they can act on

  • For the ownerA mid-size aggregator-dependent restaurant is losing on the order of thousands a year to commission it could keep. Bleed sizes it in a minute, for free, with the maths shown.
  • ModelFree audit as the wedge. Paid tier for the agency: batch a whole postcode from OpenStreetMap, white-label the dossier, track which leads converted.
  • Why nowAggregator dependence is at a peak and margin pressure is real. The data to price it is public. Nobody is pointing it at the owner.
Ethics: robots.txt respected, no aggregator scraped, audited businesses never named publicly

Roadmap

What comes next

  • NextBrowser tier in the recon so JavaScript-rendered sites return a full report. Today they return a thin one, and the report says so.
  • ThenBeyond restaurants: dentists, gyms, salons. Each trade needs its own calibrated constants, which means its own field study. No claims until that work is done.
  • ThenBatch mode for agencies: a postcode in, a ranked list of leads out, each with its dossier.
  • AlwaysEvery new constant carries a citable source and a date, or it does not compute euros.
